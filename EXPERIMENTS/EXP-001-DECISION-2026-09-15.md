## Decision

EXP-001 remains open at the target boundary and must **not** advance to EXP-002 yet.

The current evidence establishes that the bridge works when loaded by a controlled Android application and that the target Minecraft installation contains ARM64 native code. It does not establish a legitimate mechanism for loading the bridge into the retail Minecraft process.

The next task is therefore a **loading-boundary research task**, not a renderer experiment.

## Required Properties of Any Future Target Method

A future method must be:

1. Authorized for the test installation and controlled by the researcher.
2. Reproducible on the documented target environment.
3. Reversible without damaging the installation or user data.
4. Observable with unedited process/log evidence.
5. Free of exploit chains, security bypasses, credential access, stealth persistence, or unauthorized process injection.
6. Free of committing or redistributing proprietary Minecraft APKs, native libraries, or copyrighted assets in this repository.

## Stop Condition

If no method satisfies these properties, the Minecraft portion of EXP-001 remains `BLOCKED / NOT TESTED`. The controlled Android harness result remains valid and should not be discarded or relabeled as Minecraft evidence.
