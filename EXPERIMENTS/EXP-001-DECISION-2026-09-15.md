## Final Decision

**EXP-001 is CLOSED at the Minecraft target boundary.**

The controlled Android evidence is valid and retained. The Minecraft-specific portion is formally classified as `BLOCKED / NOT TESTED` because no authorized, reproducible, reversible, observable target-side loading path has been established.

This closure is not a claim that integration is impossible. It means the current experiment has reached the evidence boundary defined by its safety and reproducibility requirements.

Android's application model uses UID/process isolation, and native-library accessibility is package-scoped. The Android documentation for `<uses-native-library>` describes making native libraries accessible to an application; it does not define that mechanism as a way to attach an unrelated library to another application's process.

## Required Properties of Any Future Target Method

A future method must be:

1. Authorized for the test installation and controlled by the researcher.
2. Reproducible on the documented target environment.
3. Reversible without damaging the installation or user data.
4. Observable with unedited process/log evidence.
5. Free of exploit chains, security bypasses, credential access, stealth persistence, or unauthorized process injection.
6. Free of committing or redistributing proprietary Minecraft APKs, native libraries, or copyrighted assets in this repository.

## EXP-002 Gate — Revised Objective Approved

EXP-002 is released from HOLD with a revised observation-first objective.

It must **not** assume that the PVR bridge executes inside retail Minecraft.

The next experiment may perform read-only target inventory, runtime observation available through normal/researcher-controlled mechanisms, public-source architecture mapping, and comparison against the controlled render-target contract.

The next experiment must stop before unauthorized process modification or security-boundary bypass.
